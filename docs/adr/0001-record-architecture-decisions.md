# ADR-0001: Record architecture decisions

* Status: Accepted

## Context
Decisions get forgotten, and "why did you do it that way?" is the most common interview question about a project.

## Decision
Every significant choice gets a short ADR using `0000-template.md`. ADRs are never edited after acceptance; they are superseded.

## Consequences
A readable history of the design, and ready-made interview answers.
